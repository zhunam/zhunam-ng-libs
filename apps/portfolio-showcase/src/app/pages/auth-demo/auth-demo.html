Live Demo

A thin, unified wrapper over Firebase Auth and Supabase Auth, reactive user state, a route guard, and ready-made login/register/reset-password forms. This demo runs against an in-memory mock, never a real Firebase or Supabase project.

Behavior is identical — switching providers doesn't require touching your application code, only the entry point you import from and provide in app.config.ts (see "Usage with form-ui" at the bottom of this page).

Reset password

Tip: submit with the demo email above, then submit again with a completely made-up address like anyone@random.com . The form's message stays identical either way, that's the anti-user-enumeration protection, not a bug. The Result panel on the right shows what's really happening underneath: a made-up email actually rejects with auth/user-not-found, the form just never lets that difference reach the screen.
